# Developer handover – preserve these product decisions
1. One person, one login identity; multiple professional capabilities.
2. Roles and permissions are assigned per matter. A role in one matter never grants access to another.
3. Conflict alert is private to the concerned professional, informational only, with no automatic disqualification or intervention.
4. Shared official repository is controlled by arbitrator/tribunal. On tribunal change, incoming arbitrator configures a new repository; do not transfer outgoing arbitrator's personal cloud account.
5. Venue and service bookings can be standalone without matter registration.
6. No payment gateway in initial launch; customer pays provider directly.
7. Arbitrator fees and third-party matter expenses are separate ledgers.
8. Typist's Minutes of Hearing draft requires arbitrator approval before circulation.
9. No automatic AI ingestion of matter repository or tribunal deliberations.
10. Do not imply institutional endorsement or verified credentials without evidence.

This starter's RLS policies are only a baseline. Add security tests for cross-matter access, invitations, role changes, provider data and administrative access before live deployment.
